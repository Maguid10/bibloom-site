Questions? We’ve got you.

Is Bibloom free?

Yes. Bibloom is free to download and gives you daily Bible verses for free. Premium unlocks unlimited verses, all seven themes, unlimited Lay it down, every background, your own photo and app icons. It starts with a 7-day free trial, and you can cancel anytime.

Which Bible translation do you use?

Bibloom uses the World English Bible (WEB), a modern public-domain translation.

How does Lay it down work?

Write or say what’s weighing on you. Bibloom finds a real Bible verse that speaks to it, with a short word of encouragement. If you’re going through something very hard, it points you to people who can help right away.

Is Bibloom on Android?

Bibloom is available on iPhone today.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Bibloom. You can cancel anytime, including during the free trial.
